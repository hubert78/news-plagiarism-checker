Methodology Deep Dive

📐

Why Cosine Similarity?

Unlike Euclidean distance, which measures the straight-line physical distance between document vectors, Cosine Similarity calculates the angle between them. This makes it invariant to document length, meaning an expanded 800-word feature story and a concise 200-word wire report are compared purely on lexical alignment.

⚖️

Smooth IDF Formulation

Matches scikit-learn's standard formula: idf(t) = ln((1 + n) / (1 + df(t))) + 1. Adding 1 to document counts prevents zero division errors for terms occurring across all documents, penalizing pervasive vocabulary while surfacing distinct investigative phrasing.

🇬🇭

Ghanaian Media Syndication

In Ghana's news ecosystem (GhanaWeb, 3News, MyJoyOnline, Modern Ghana, Yen), outlets frequently syndicate GNA press releases with minimal modifications. Setting the threshold to 10%–15% effectively detects paraphrased wire stories, whereas 70%+ flags verbatim republished duplicates.
