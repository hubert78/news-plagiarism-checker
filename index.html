Methodology

This application analyzes text similarity across five major Ghanaian news portals: Ghanaweb, 3News, MyJoyOnline, Modern Ghana, and Yen Ghana.

Detection Steps

  1. Text cleaning: HTML removal, punctuation stripping, and lowercasing.
  2. Stopword removal and morphological Porter Stemming.
  3. TF-IDF Vectorization with L2 normalization matching scikit-learn.
  4. Cosine similarity matrix dot products.
